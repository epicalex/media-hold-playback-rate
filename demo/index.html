Media Chrome gesture experiment

Hold to choose and lock playback speed

Long press the video to temporarily play faster. While holding, slide vertically to step through speeds, slide right to lock, and slide left to unlock.

Read the GitHub README

1

Hold to use the last chosen speed.

2

Slide up or down to reveal the speed stack.

3

Slide right to lock; slide left to unlock.

The element dispatches Media Chrome mediaplaybackraterequest events instead of mutating the video directly.

Thresholds are configured as attributes on <media-hold-playback-rate>. 

<media-controller>
  <video slot="media" src="video.mp4" playsinline></video>

  <media-hold-playback-rate
    hold-delay="280"
    vertical-threshold="24"
    horizontal-threshold="72"
  >
    <button slot="rate" value="1.25">1.25x</button>
    <button slot="rate" value="1.5">1.5x</button>
    <button slot="rate" value="1.75">1.75x</button>
    <button slot="rate" value="2">2x</button>
  </media-hold-playback-rate>

  <media-control-bar>
    <media-play-button></media-play-button>
    <media-time-range></media-time-range>
    <media-playback-rate-button></media-playback-rate-button>
  </media-control-bar>
</media-controller>
